Invasion of the Turkeys

Thanksgiving will never be the same.

💾 New save — progress, coins & upgrades save automatically on this device as you play.

The turkeys have had enough — this year they're coming for us, wave after wave across 12 levels, from the barnyard to the Final Feast, every flock stranger than the last. You're Earth's last line of defense. Man the cranberry cannon!

How to Play

🖐️ Drag on the field to move — your thumbs never leave the action
🔴 Your cranberry cannon fires on its own — fast, snappy shots; just aim by moving
💣 The Gravy Bomb pad (bottom-right of the field) blasts everything on screen
🌽 Grab corn for points and coins, cranberries for triple-shot, and pie heals a flat +1 ♥ — a missed pie lands and waits on the ground for you (max 2 at once, and no pie drops during a boss fight)
🧬 Mutated turkeys creep in from Level 4, more every level — extra heads, extra tough, bonus coins. Every turkey bursts into feathers, potatoes & gravy
🪙 Turkeys drop coins — after each run you return to the Lobby to upgrade your cannon
🦃 Clear every wave, then beat the boss turkey
⚠️ Bosses dive all the way down to your row — watch for the dashed red lane, then move out of it. Sometimes the boss and its flock dive together to trap you, and every fight gets meaner the longer it lasts and the lower the boss's health drops

Choose Your Chapter

Beat a level to unlock the next. Replay any unlocked level to farm coins for the Workshop.

🎁 Harvest Chests

Every chapter you clear drops a Harvest Chest from its boss. Bigger chapters drop rarer chests — FEAST chests hide coin jackpots. Chests are earned by playing only.

🏆 Leaderboard

Your best finished runs, saved on this device. A run joins the board when it ends — game over or total victory. Stars, achievements and chapter bests live in the Lobby's 🏆 Trophy.

What you see is what you play — literally the same drawings: the trailer, the title screen, the Lobby and the game field all draw one cannon (dark-iron barrel, cranberry muzzle ring, gold bands, spoked wheels, pilgrim hat), one turkey design per flock, and one Big Gobbler, from a single shared art source. No screen swaps in a different-looking version.

Drag on the field to move. The Gravy Bomb pad sits in the bottom-right corner of the field. On a keyboard: ← → or A / D to move, B or Shift for a Gravy Bomb, P to pause. Twelve levels stand between Earth and the turkeys — each one has more waves than the last and ends with a boss. Coins you collect carry back to the Lobby, where the cannon gets its upgrades. This is a grind: expect retries. Your progress, coins and upgrades save automatically on this device — and your best finished runs land on the Leaderboard in the main menu.
